App & program FAQs
What is Reverse Health?

Reverse Health is a personalized wellness app designed specifically for women over 40. Our mission is to help reverse age-related weight gain and improve energy, mood, and overall well-being.

Tailored to the unique needs of midlife women
Combines nutrition, fitness, mindset, and lifestyle changes
Focuses on sustainable, holistic transformation, not quick fixes
Who is Reverse Health for?

Reverse Health is designed for women 40+ who:

Struggle with weight gain or plateaus despite trying other programs
Experience hormonal changes like menopause or perimenopause
Need a plan that fits into their real-life routine, not 2-hour gym sessions

Whether you’re new to fitness or starting over, Reverse Health adjusts to you.

How does Reverse Health work?

Here’s how it works:

Take a short quiz to assess your current health, goals, and lifestyle
Get a custom plan with personalized workouts, meals, and daily habits
Download the app and follow your daily modules
Track your progress inside your dashboard
What features are included in the Reverse Health app?

The app gives you an all-in-one system to feel strong, confident, and healthy again:

Low-impact home workouts like wall pilates and chair yoga
Meal plans based on your body and preferences
Mindset coaching and habit tracking
Progress dashboards, expert content, and community support
Why do I need a specific plan for women 40+?

Whether you’re new to fitness or starting over, Reverse Health adjusts to you.

Estrogen levels drop, leading to more body fat and less muscle
Metabolism slows, making weight loss harder
Bone density and joint health become more important

Reverse Health works with your body’s changes using age-appropriate strength training, balanced nutrition, and stress support.

How does this fit into my very busy lifestyle?

We built Reverse Health to support busy women.

Workouts are 15–20 minutes, 3–4 times a week
Do everything from home, no gym required
Pause and resume workouts as needed to match your schedule

Is Reverse Health safe for women with health conditions?

Reverse Health is built to be low-impact and flexible.

Suitable for joint sensitivity and physical limitations
Nutrition plans are balanced and not extreme
Always check with your doctor before starting if you have a health condition
