App & program FAQs

What is Reverse Health?

Reverse Health is a personalized wellness app designed specifically for women over 40. Our mission is to help reverse age-related weight gain and improve energy, mood, and overall well-being.

  1. Tailored to the unique needs of midlife women
  2. Combines nutrition, fitness, mindset, and lifestyle changes
  3. Focuses on sustainable, holistic transformation, not quick fixes

Who is Reverse Health for?

Reverse Health is designed for women 40+ who:

  1. Struggle with weight gain or plateaus despite trying other programs
  2. Experience hormonal changes like menopause or perimenopause
  3. Need a plan that fits into their real-life routine, not 2-hour gym sessions

Whether you’re new to fitness or starting over, Reverse Health adjusts to you.

How does Reverse Health work?

Here’s how it works:

  1. Take a short quiz to assess your current health, goals, and lifestyle
  2. Get a custom plan with personalized workouts, meals, and daily habits
  3. Download the app and follow your daily modules
  4. Track your progress inside your dashboard

What features are included in the Reverse Health app?

The app gives you an all-in-one system to feel strong, confident, and healthy again:

  1. Low-impact home workouts like wall pilates and chair yoga
  2. Meal plans based on your body and preferences
  3. Mindset coaching and habit tracking
  4. Progress dashboards, expert content, and community support

Why do I need a specific plan for women 40+?

Whether you’re new to fitness or starting over, Reverse Health adjusts to you.

  1. Estrogen levels drop, leading to more body fat and less muscle
  2. Metabolism slows, making weight loss harder
  3. Bone density and joint health become more important

Reverse Health works with your body’s changes using age-appropriate strength training, balanced nutrition, and stress support.

How does this fit into my very busy lifestyle?

We built Reverse Health to support busy women.

  1. Workouts are 15–20 minutes, 3–4 times a week
  2. Do everything from home, no gym required
  3. Pause and resume workouts as needed to match your schedule

How much does it cost and how do I sign up?

To see your personalized plan and pricing, click the Join Reverse Health button and complete our short quiz.

  1. Pricing varies depending on your location, language, and selected plan
  2. At the end of the quiz, your custom plan and pricing will be revealed
  3. Start your quiz now to see your tailored offer

Is Reverse Health safe for women with health conditions?

Reverse Health is built to be low-impact and flexible.

  1. Suitable for joint sensitivity and physical limitations
  2. Nutrition plans are balanced and not extreme
  3. Always check with your doctor before starting if you have a health condition

Is Reverse Health the best weight loss app for women over 40?

Many of our users believe so, because:

  1. It’s specifically made for women over 40
  2. Addresses hormonal, metabolic, and lifestyle changes
  3. Offers expert guidance, habit coaching, and flexible workouts
